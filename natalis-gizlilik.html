Giriş

Bu politika, Natalis mobil uygulamasının (Android ve iOS, paket kimliği: com.natalis.app) verilerinizi nasıl işlediğini açıklar. Natalis; doğum haritası, transitler, burç uyumu ve astroloji yorumları sunar. Uygulamada üyelik, giriş ekranı veya arka uç sunucusu yoktur; tüm astronomik hesaplamalar cihazınızda yapılır.

Kısaca: Girdiğiniz doğum bilgileri yalnızca telefonunuzda saklanır; bize veya herhangi bir sunucuya gönderilmez, satılmaz ve reklam hedeflemesinde kullanılmaz. Uygulamadaki reklamları Google AdMob gösterir.

Natalis: Birth Chart Astrology — Privacy Policy

Package: com.natalis.app Effective date: November 10, 2026 Version: 1.0

Summary

Natalis has no account, no sign-in and no backend server. All astronomical calculations run on your device. The information you enter (profile name or nickname, birth date, birth time, birth city and its coordinates), your Stardust balance, unlocked Cosmic Seals and your settings (language, house system, sound) are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or any server, never sold, and never used for ad targeting. The app works offline; the internet is used only to load ads.

Sharing

Share buttons (for example in "Life Trailer") pass the selected text to your phone's share sheet. It only reaches the app or person you choose; Natalis does not upload it anywhere.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows Google AdMob ads (banner, interstitial and optional rewarded video). AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device and app information, ad interactions and crash/diagnostic data to serve, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, ads are personalized or not according to the choice you make in Google's consent form (UMP); you can change it via "Ad privacy options" in the 🌐 menu in the app's top bar. Your birth data and astrology results are never shared with AdMob or any ad network and are not used for ad targeting. See how Google uses data and the Google Privacy Policy. On iPhone the app does not request Apple's tracking (IDFA) permission; ads are shown without the advertising identifier.

Data Deletion

You can edit or delete profiles at any time in Manage profiles; uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes everything. We keep no server-side copy.

Entertainment Purpose & Children

Astrology readings in Natalis are for self-reflection, entertainment and information only and are not medical, legal, financial or psychological advice. The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your Rights & Changes

Your data stays under your control. For requests under GDPR or Turkey's KVKK, contact us. If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
